Conclusion

This study shows that architecture choice significantly affects audio deepfake detection performance using FFT-derived STFT features. The dashboard conclusion is generated from the exported notebook results.

Limitations and Future Work

Appropriate scope for a mini project.

Limitations

The project uses the for-2sec subset and should be tested on more realistic audio conditions in future work. Results may change when training on more samples or when evaluated on external datasets.

Future Work

Future versions can test for-rerec audio, train on larger samples, try CNN-GRU hybrids, explore self-attention, and deploy a real upload-and-predict demo.
